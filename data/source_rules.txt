What this is
4,355 real carbon credit projects from the Berkeley Voluntary Registry Offsets Database (v2026-06) that still have unretired credits. Each row is one project.
Databse columns: registry, status, scope, project_type, reduction_or_removal, region, country, developer, vintage_year, available_tonnes, has_buffer_pool, had_reversal.
Fake columns: price_usd_per_t and risk_rating. These are NOT market data.

Columns
available_tonnes: credits issued and not yet retired. You cannot buy more than this per project.
price_usd_per_t: synthetic price per tonne CO2e, loosely based on typical ranges by project type, with vintage, quality and random noise.
risk_rating: a noisy third-party style rating, AAA (safest) to CCC (riskiest). About 12% of projects are unrated. Ratings are imperfect: treat them as evidence, not truth.
has_buffer_pool: whether the project deposited credits in a registry buffer pool (an insurance mechanism). Real data.
had_reversal: whether the project has had a recorded reversal. Real data.

Rules of thumb
Credits can fail (reversal, downgrade, invalidation). Failures are correlated: projects in the same country, with the same developer, registry or project type tend to fail together.
If had_reversal = Yes, failure probability is multiplied by 1.5x (capped at 100%)
If has_buffer_pool = Yes, 50% of credit losses are recovered in the event of a failure
Base failure probability
AAA: 1% | AA: 2% | A: 4% | BBB: 7% | BB: 12% | B: 20% | CCC: 35% | NaN: 15%
Total spending cannot exceed $1,000,000.
You may purchase any quantity up to a project's `available_tonnes`.
